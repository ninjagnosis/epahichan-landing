## Your account

- You must be 18 or older.
- Sign in is by your mobile number and a code sent by SMS. Keep your phone and your unlock method (fingerprint, face or passcode) to yourself.
- Give true details. A certificate application with false details will be refused or revoked.
- You are responsible for what is done with your account and your signing key. Tell us and NCC promptly if your phone is lost or you think someone else has used your key.

## Your certificate

- Your certificate is issued, suspended, renewed and revoked by NCC under its own policy and the Electronic Transactions Act, 2063. We pass your application to NCC and show you its result.
- Fees for a certificate or its renewal are shown in the app before you pay. They are paid through Fonepay or a bank or wallet app.
- A certificate that has expired or been revoked cannot be used to sign.

## Signing documents

- A signature you make is made with your certificate and binds you in the way the law gives it effect. Read a document before you sign it.
- A digital signature is valid for a legal signature requirement only when made by the procedures in the Act and its Rules, and only for documents the Act covers. Some documents are outside the Act, for example deeds that transfer title to immovable property, wills, negotiable instruments and powers of attorney. Do not use ePahichan to sign those and expect the Act to apply.
- A signature shows who signed what. It does not by itself show when. Do not rely on a signing time shown on a device as proof of time.
- Signed documents you send to others can be kept by them, and we cannot recall them.

## Using the API

- API access is given to an organization after NCC onboards it. Keep your keys on your server and never in an app or a public repository.
- Test requests in the sandbox are not legal signatures.
- You may not use the ID check API to look up people who have not asked you to, or to collect records. Each check is for a person who is dealing with you.
